## Why the drill is not passed

A review on 2026-11-14 read all 24 val rollouts and the code. The low pass rate is mostly the
harness, not the models:

- **Wiki task (0/6): unpassable.** `task.json` names a skill, `greenlight-wiki`, that does not
  exist; missing skills were dropped silently; the rollout prompt gave four field names and no
  output schema. Every wiki rollout since M1 failed on output shape.
- **Loop task (0/6): the success condition was not derivable.** The brief says "get it onto beta";
  nothing in the fixture or the skill says that means pushing `develop`. The skill says
  `pnpm greenlight`, and the sandbox has no `pnpm`. No rollout failed the ordering the task tests.
- **The step limit was invisible** to the agent, and requests spent on retries were graded as
  failures.
- **Replay tasks (1/12)** are the only results that reflect model ability, and their graders
  reject some defensible solutions.
- An earlier defect of the same kind was found on 2026-11-12: the val loop fixture failed its own
  preview gate (`bench/references/loop/reference-gates.test.ts` now guards it).

The gate also cannot decide soundly on this bench: two objective val tasks make the bootstrap a
two-valued rule, no replay task passes reliably so the safety constraint is vacuous, and one flaky
replay blocks every high-radius patch.

## What M1 still needs

1. The bench fixes above, then a new train run and val baseline.
2. The gate fixes (per-skill slice, an exact test, an explicit underpowered outcome).
3. CI green on the branch, including `bench check-graders` and the docker isolation suite.
4. A drill run by the definition in 13 §6, recorded here as a new file.
