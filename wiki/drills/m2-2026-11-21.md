## The criteria

1. **The maintainer role passes the `wiki/probe-layer-incidents` grader: not met.** When this
   record was first written, 06 §10.2 step 2 said the iteration's own maintainer output is graded
   by that task. `bench iterate` never did that, and on the current wiki it could not do it
   soundly. The grader's rubric expects the 06 §10.1 wiki state, three hypothesis pages with no
   conditional rule, and it fails an output that writes no conditional pattern. Earlier iterations
   have already compiled that rule into `pattern/probe-touches-wrong-layer`, so the correct output
   on today's wiki is the one the maintainer gave ("no page changes"), and the rubric would fail
   it. The spec now states the criterion as something an iteration can be checked against: the val
   task, whose fixture is the 10.1 state, is baseline-passing on the frontier model, which is 4 of
   5 runs. The hand-off prints the role's graded results beside the maintainer pass. In this
   baseline the frontier model passed that task in 2 of 5 runs and the small model in 0 of 5, so
   the criterion is not met, and the number to move is the frontier model's 2 of 5.
2. **A valid proposal or a `no_action` with a reason: met.** The `no_action` cites six raw reads
   and gives its reason. The greenlight-wiki slice is 5/5 and 2/5 at the frontier baseline, so the
   smallest p a candidate can reach is 0.083, above alpha 0.05, and the gate would return
   `underpowered` whatever the rollouts did. Neither bundle holds val or test material.
3. **The proposal was evaluated by its outlet's rule: met.** A `no_action` has no outlet
   evaluation. It is recorded as a ledger event with its reason.
4. **The control is rejected because the Neon replay regresses: met.** `controlOutcome` is
   `met`. The reasons include `replay-regression` on `replay/idle-probe-leaves-store-asleep-val`
   on both models, baseline 5/5 against candidate 0/5, where 4/5 is needed. Non-regression over
   the four other objective tasks was flat: 17/20 against 17/20 on sonnet (p 0.738) and 9/20
   against 9/20 on haiku (p 0.641), so the rejection comes from the replay alone.
5. **Both ledger events exist and reproduce: met.** `greenlight bench gate --recompute
   5795207592826a3e4e2ee9c510f26200228490e5a01a3eb7c72c60cb0caddc06` recomputes `reject` and
   matches event `58a33f84…`. The `no_action` has no gate input to recompute.
6. **The owner has reviewed the ledger rows: met by delegation.** Both rows were reviewed under
   the owner's standing instruction for this work, and the reviewer field says so. The
   `no_action` row reads `reject` because the review command then applied one decision to every
   row of the iteration. The command now records a control row as `reject` and leaves the
   proposal's row to the owner's decision (`rowDecision`, `packages/wiki/src/bench/iterate/review.ts`).
   The existing row stands, since the ledger is append-only.
7. **The cost of the iteration is reported: met in part.** When this record was first written
   `bench report` was not built. It is now, and `greenlight bench report --metric cost` gives the
   iteration's one new evaluation, the control candidate `675b8dbc…`, as 50 rollouts and 109.3
   rollout-minutes, on a reused baseline of 50 rollouts and 114.5 rollout-minutes. The ledger's

   harness, so their requests, tokens and dollars are unmeasured, and the report says so instead
